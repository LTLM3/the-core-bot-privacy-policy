THE CORE Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how THE CORE Discord bot (“the Bot”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when used inside Discord servers.

1. Information We Collect

THE CORE bot may collect and store limited Discord-related data required to operate community systems, including:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord server/guild IDs
  • Role and access status
  • Interaction records from buttons, menus, and modals
  • Merit, leaderboard, event, check-in, and objective submission records
  • Soldier number, connection code claim status, and related account-linking records
  • Operational logs needed to diagnose errors and maintain the Bot

If users submit text through bot-controlled forms, modals, or objective workflows, that submitted content may be stored as part of the relevant community system.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used only to operate THE CORE community features, including:

  • Managing role-based access and initiation flows
  • Tracking merits, objectives, event participation, and leaderboards
  • Assigning or reclaiming Soldier Numbers
  • Maintaining Digital Soldier inventory and account-linking systems
  • Providing moderation, review, and operator tools
  • Debugging errors, preventing abuse, and maintaining service reliability

3. Message Content

THE CORE bot may access message content only where required for bot features, such as objective submissions, command handling, review workflows, or other server systems that rely on user-submitted text.

Message content is not sold and is not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

4. Guild Members Data

THE CORE bot uses Discord server member data to check roles, manage access, process initiation, remove expired access roles, and support server-specific community features.

5. Presence Data

THE CORE bot does not currently use Discord Presence data and does not track user online status, activity status, or rich presence information.

6. Data Storage

Some information is stored outside of Discord in databases and infrastructure used to operate the Bot. This may include hosting providers, database services, and storage providers used by THE CORE.

We take reasonable steps to protect stored data, including restricting access to authorized operators and using infrastructure providers that support encryption and access controls.

7. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as needed to operate THE CORE community systems, maintain historical records such as merits and Soldier Numbers, comply with moderation or security needs, or resolve technical issues.

Operational logs may be retained for a shorter period unless needed for debugging, security, or abuse prevention.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. Data is not shared with third parties except where required to operate the Bot, comply with legal obligations, prevent abuse, or use hosting/database infrastructure providers.

9. User Controls and Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of personal data associated with THE CORE bot by contacting us at:

Email: support@ltarmy.com

Some records, such as moderation, anti-abuse, security, transaction-related, or historical community integrity records, may need to be retained where reasonably necessary.

10. Children

THE CORE bot is intended for use within Discord communities and is not directed toward children under the age required by Discord’s Terms of Service.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact:

Email: support@ltarmy.com
